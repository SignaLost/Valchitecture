# How do the build camera and workbenches work together?

The build camera unlocks at level 50 (Raven's View). In Build or Edit mode, press
`Alt + B` to turn it on or off. Until level 100 it needs a workbench, or another crafting
station like a forge, to work from.

## Where the camera can go

- **Level 50.** Stand inside a workbench's range. The camera can fly around that
  workbench, a little past its edge, or a short distance around you (7 m at level 50,
  growing to 16 m by level 74).
- **Level 75 (Bifröst Step).** The camera can fly from one workbench to the next, and
  standing near a workbench's edge is enough to let it out.
- **Level 100 (Raven's Reach).** No workbench is needed.

Your character stands still while the camera is out, and the camera can't fly too high
or too far away from you.

If you enter a mode with no workbench nearby, the camera stays with your character until
you press the camera key. Walking into a workbench's range later won't pull it away.

## Which workbench counts for building

Pieces that need a workbench follow the game's usual rule: your character must stand
inside the range of one. That workbench's ring shows white, and any other workbench the
camera is using shows red.

At level 100, a workbench near the camera counts too, so you can build at a distant base.
The same goes for removing, moving and repairing pieces.

## Reach

In a mode you can build 1.5 times farther than normal. From level 60 your reach keeps
growing, up to double at level 100 (the `Longer Reach` setting under `2. Abilities`).
Reach is measured from your character, even when the camera is out. At level 100 with the
camera at a workbench, you can build anywhere inside that workbench's range.
