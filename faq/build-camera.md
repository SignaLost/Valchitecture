# How do the build camera and workbenches work together?

## Where the camera can go

The camera unlocks at level 50 and needs a crafting station to work from until 100.

- **Level 50 to 74.** You must be standing inside a station's range. The camera works
  from that station and can fly within its range plus 5 m, or within its own range of
  you (7 m at level 50, 16 m at 74).
- **Level 75 (Bifröst Step).** The working station is the one the camera is inside, or
  within 5 m of, so the camera can move from station to station. It can also fly within
  your build reach plus 5 m of you, and standing within 5 m of a station's edge is enough
  to let it out.
- **Level 100.** No station is needed.

Around a station the camera can rise to 1.5 times that station's range, and it always
stays close enough to you that your character's area of the world stays loaded. Your
character stands still while the camera is out.

If you enter a mode below level 100 with no station available, the camera stays on your
character until you press the camera key, so walking into a station later does not pull
the camera away.

## Which station counts for building

Pieces that need a workbench or other station follow the game's rule: you must be
standing inside a station of that kind. That station's ring shows white; any other
station the camera works from shows red.

From level 100 (Raven's Reach), a station whose range reaches within 5 m of the camera
also counts, so you can build around a distant base. While you stand outside every
station of that kind, such pieces can only be placed inside a station's range; elsewhere
the game reports a missing station. The same rules apply to removing, moving in Edit
mode and area repair.

## Reach

Build reach is 1.5 times the game's in a mode, and grows another 20% every ten levels
from 60, to double at 100 (the `Longer Reach` setting). Reach is measured from your
character, as the game measures it. At level 100 with the camera at a station, you can
build anywhere inside that station's range instead.
