# How does moving pieces in Edit mode work?

Edit mode unlocks at level 25 (Huginn's Counsel). Select the hammer's Repair tool and
press the mode key (`Shift + B`). Click a piece to pick it up, then rotate, nudge and snap
it as usual. Click again to set it down, or right-click to put it back where it was.

## What can be moved

You can pick up any piece the hammer could remove, so wards, no-build zones and the
workbench rule still apply. You can also move a private chest with items in it, even
though the game won't let you remove one. Where you set a piece down must follow the
game's usual placement rules.

You can't move a chest someone has open, or a ship with players aboard. With `Guard Used`
on (under `7. Building Rules`, on by default), nobody can move or remove a cart someone is
pulling, a seat someone sits in or a bed someone sleeps in.

## What a move keeps and costs

A moved piece keeps everything: chest contents, smelter progress, who built it and the
skill it was built with. A move costs the stamina of two hammer swings and wears the
hammer like removing and placing a piece. It also raises your skill a little.

While you hold a piece, anything resting on it stays up.
